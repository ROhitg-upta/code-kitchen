## 1. Event Concept
* **Event Name:** **`CODE LOCKOUT: The 180-Minute Kitchen Sprint`**
* **Theme / Concept:** *"High-Pressure Culinary Coding Arena."*  
  Traditional 3-hour college contests suffer from a massive flaw: 70% of 1st and 2nd-year students get stuck on Problem 1 in the first 40 minutes, lose motivation, and mentally check out. **Code Lockout** transforms the seminar hall into an electric, 3-stage interactive arena combining **Rapid Bug-Bounty (The Prep Station)**, **1v1 Grid Claiming (The Main Course)**, and **Strategic Power-Cards (Chef’s Special)**.
* **Core Objectives:**
  1. **100% Hall Retention for 180 Minutes:** Give both beginners (debugging/output prediction) and advanced coders (algorithmic optimization) clear paths to score points.
  2. **Zero-Chaos Execution with 5 Volunteers:** Automate check-ins, live scoring, and digital certificates via our custom **Code Kitchen (`ChefOps v2.6`)** platform.
* **Target Audience:** 1st, 2nd, and 3rd-year B.Tech students across CSE, CSE-AIML, CSE-DS, IT, and ECE (**80–120 students competing in Pairs of 2 = 40 to 60 Squads**).

## 5. Problem Solving (2 Real Campus Crisis Scenarios)

### Crisis 1: Seminar Hall Wi-Fi Collapses When 100 Students Connect
* **Mitigation & Instant Fix:**
  1. **50% Device Reduction by Design:** Squads of 2 mean only **50 laptops** (not 100) are active.
  2. **Isolated Console Network:** Volunteer #3’s projector laptop runs on a dedicated 5G hotspot (Jio + Airtel backup) so the main scoreboard never drops.
  3. **Offline USB + Mobile Data Fallback:** All Round 1 & 2 problems are pre-loaded as lightweight offline HTML/PDF packets on 3 USB drives and projected on the dual screens; squads submit lightweight text tokens via mobile data (`<50 KB`), keeping the 180-minute clock running with zero delay.

### Crisis 2: Late Arrivals & Last-Minute No-Shows
* **Mitigation & Instant Fix:**
  1. **Controlled Overbooking (120 Cap for 100 Seats):** Absorbs the standard 15% college no-show rate.
  2. **Gamified Punctuality (`+50 Early-Bird Kitchen Credits`):** Squads scanned at the QR gate at least 5 minutes before kick-off start Round 1 with **+50 Bonus Kitchen Credits** (usable for finale hints). This eliminates late walk-ins and gets 90%+ students seated ahead of time.
