5. Problem Solving & Crisis Playbooks Ground Realism

Problem 1: Seminar Hall Wi-Fi Collapses Under 100 Concurrent Connections

  • Why it happens: Campus access points often throttle when 100+ laptops/phones hit an external coding platform simultaneously.
  • Our 3-Layer Solution:
    1. Architectural Load Halving: Squads of 2 mean only 50 active laptops are competing, reducing Wi-Fi load by 50% from Minute 0.
    2. Dedicated Console Link: Volunteer #3’s projector laptop runs on an isolated 5G mobile hotspot (Jio + Airtel dual-SIM backup) so the main screen and live leaderboard never freeze.
    3. Offline Local Mirror Fallback: All Round 1 & Round 2 problem sets are pre-cached as lightweight offline HTML/PDF packets on our local server and 3 USB drives. Even if internet drops completely, problems remain projected on the dual screens and squads submit lightweight answer tokens via mobile 4G/5G with zero downtime.

Problem 2: Late Walk-Ins & Last-Minute No-Shows Threaten the 3-Hour Schedule

  • Why it happens: In free/low-cost college events, 15–20% of registrants don't show up, and 30% walk in 20 minutes late, delaying Round 1.
  • Our 3-Layer Solution:
    1. Controlled Overbooking (125 Passes Issued for 100 Target): We accept 120–125 registrations + maintain an automated Priority Waitlist to absorb the standard 15% campus no-show rate and guarantee a packed hall of 100+ students.
    2. Gamified On-Time Incentive ("+50 Early-Bird Kitchen Credits"): We announce 2 hours prior on WhatsApp that every squad scanned at the QR gate before T-00:05 automatically starts Round 1 with +50 Bonus Kitchen Credits. Since credits buy hints in the finale, 90%+ squads arrive 10 minutes early!
